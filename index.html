— 2.2 m · FAQ

Before you jump in.

What to bring

A swimsuit and a bottle of water. We supply all the aquatic equipment.

Min height150 cm
Max weight130 kg
Do I need to know how to swim?
No. Every exercise is done standing in the pool or riding an underwater bike.
Are the classes right for me?
Yes. Beginners, athletes, seniors, pregnant women and people recovering from injury all take part, and we adjust the moves for each person.
Is there an age or size limit?
There's no age limit. For safety on the bikes we recommend a minimum height of 150 cm and a maximum weight of 130 kg.
How soon will I see results?
Most people notice improvements after 3 to 5 sessions. Bigger changes come with regular classes.
Is aqua fitness good during pregnancy?
It can ease aches and swelling, improve circulation, help lower the risk of gestational diabetes and support recovery after birth. Check with your doctor first and tell your coach.
Do you offer personal training?
Yes, one-to-one or in pairs on the aqua bikes, at our partner clubs and hotels or in your own private pool.
Can I share my package?
No. Packages are for one person only.
How do I book?
Message us on WhatsApp, book through the website, or contact the partner hotel or club directly.
